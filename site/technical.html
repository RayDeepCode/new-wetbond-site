Repair

Use

Note

Product characteristics

  • Two-part material, mixed on site to a published ratio
  • Water-reactive: engineered to go after the water in concrete; applied to pre-wetted concrete, standing water removed first
  • Applied by roller, brush or squeegee, at a rate set by the substrate's porosity
  • UV stable by chemistry: WetBond UV and WetBond Color UV are aliphatic, so sunlight doesn’t yellow or break them down
  • Color available: WetBond Color and WetBond Color UV are tinted; the deepest-penetrating products stay unpigmented
  • Compatible topcoats: epoxy and polyaspartic floor finishes bond to WetBond; compatible topcoats applied inside its cure window bond to it chemically, which lets moist slabs be primed and finished
  • Repairs: cracks, joints and tie-rod holes are primed with WetBond, filled while the primer is tacky, then treated over; active leaks are stopped with Injection Gel first

Fastening into treated concrete

Standard fastening practice applies to WetBond-treated walls and slabs with one change: any fastener that penetrates past the treated depth must be sealed. Drive the fastener, withdraw it, dip it in WetBond, and reinstall.

Where practical, attach plates, studs and furring with WetBond Adhesive instead of mechanical fasteners to keep the treated zone unbroken.
